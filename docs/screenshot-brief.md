## Before you start

1. **Dark theme, default Obsidian.** Matches the hero and the brand assets.
2. **Zoom the app to ~120%** (`Cmd +` twice). Small type is unreadable at
   GitHub's column width.
3. **Window about 1400 wide.** Wide enough for the pane plus a note.
4. **Sidebar pane open** on the right.
5. Capture with `Shift-Cmd-4`, then `Space`, then click the window: macOS
   captures just that window, at retina resolution.

Send them as they are. Do not crop or resize; I will do that.

## 6. A conflict

The one capture needing setup, and the most valuable after the hero, because
"zero silent overwrites" is the claim that separates Havemind from every other
sync tool.

To make one:
1. Turn the phone to airplane mode.
2. Edit the **same line** of the same note on both devices, differently.
3. Turn the phone back on and wait a few seconds.
4. A conflict copy lands in `Havemind Conflicts/` and the pane shows an alarm
   block above the tabs.

Capture the alarm block **and** the conflict file open, if both fit.
